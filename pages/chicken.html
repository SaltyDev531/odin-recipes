Creamy Garlic Parmesan Chicken Pasta

Description:

A rich and comforting pasta dish featuring tender chicken, a creamy garlic Parmesan sauce, and perfectly cooked fettuccine. It's ideal for a satisfying weeknight dinner.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz fettuccine pasta
  • 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Steps

  1. Cook the fettuccine according to the package directions. Drain and set aside.
  2. Season the chicken with salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning.
  3. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat and cook the chicken until golden and fully cooked. Remove from the skillet.
  4. In the same skillet, sauté the garlic for about 30 seconds until fragrant.
  5. Stir in the heavy cream and bring it to a gentle simmer.
  6. Add the Parmesan cheese and stir until the sauce is smooth.
  7. Return the chicken to the skillet and mix well.
  8. Toss the cooked pasta with the sauce until evenly coated.
  9. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve immediately.
