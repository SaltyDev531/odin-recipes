Vegetable Stir-Fry with Sesame Ginger Sauce

Description:

A colorful and healthy stir-fry packed with crisp vegetables and coated in a savory sesame ginger sauce. Serve it over rice or noodles for a complete meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 carrot, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup snap peas
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp water
  • Sesame seeds for garnish

Steps

  1. Whisk together the soy sauce, sesame oil, honey, cornstarch, and water in a small bowl.
  2. Heat the vegetable oil in a large wok or skillet over high heat.
  3. Add the garlic and ginger, cooking for about 30 seconds.
  4. Stir-fry the broccoli, bell pepper, carrot, and snap peas for 4 - 6 minutes until crisp-tender.
  5. Pour the prepared sauce over the vegetables.
  6. Stir continuously until the sauce thickens and coats the vegetables.
  7. Remove from the heat and garnish with sesame seeds.
  8. Serve hot with steamed rice or noodles.
